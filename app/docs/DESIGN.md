## Brand & Style

The brand merges the precision of sovereign SecOps command with the poise and gravitas of classic royal atelier bindings. Built for senior security directors, CISOs, and enterprise remediation engineers, the design system treats enterprise vulnerability intelligence not as utilitarian noise, but as high-stakes statecraft and sovereign duty. 

The aesthetic style is **Tactile Luxury Editorial meets High-Order Modernism**. It balances warm, glowing alabaster parchment backdrops with profound imperial navy structures, illuminated by delicate gilded brass lines and lacquer-sealed severity seals. The tactile sensation evokes crisp hot-pressed heavy cotton paper, hand-tooled leather ledger trims, and burnished metallic filigree. Interfaces feel composed, calm under severe incidents, meticulously curated, and uncompromisingly authoritative.

## Colors

The palette employs a regal, high-contrast chiaroscuro foundation of layered creams and imperial midnight tones.

- **Primary (`#0B192C` - Imperial Midnight Navy):** Anchors high-hierarchy typographic headings, primary button states, navigation towers, and primary metrics. Tints into `#132440` for structural containers and active row states.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Sovereign Gilded Brass):** Emphasizes security certifications, active focal states, key metric counters, and gilded borders. Accented by deeper antique bronze (`#B8860B`) for contrast-compliant text and `#C59B27` for micro-highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#9B111E` - Crimson Lacquer):** Dedicated strictly to zero-day exposures, critical CVE vectors, and imperative remediation notices. Elevated with `#C0392B` for hover/interactive alerts.
- **Neutral (`#F5F0E6` - Antique Cream Base):** The foundation palette contains Alabaster White (`#FFFDFA`) for card faces, Warm Antique Cream (`#F5F0E6`) for canvas backgrounding, and Pressed Parchment (`#E8DFD1`) for structural hairline borders and inner wells.
- **Supportive Status Tints:** Royal Jade (`#1B4D3E`) for verified patches; Burnished Amber (`#B26A00`) for pending evaluations; Lapis Slate (`#2C3E50`) for informational telemetry.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs classical Renaissance proportions with modern computational precision:

- **EB Garamond (Display & Section Headers):** Delivers stately, bookish poise reminiscent of royal charters and timeless publications. Use medium-to-semibold weights to prevent hairline erosion on high-DPI displays.
- **Source Sans 3 (Operational UI & Body):** High-legibility humanist sans-serif delivering fatigue-free clarity across dense vulnerability reports, descriptions, and audit logs.
- **JetBrains Mono (Technical Telemetry & Labels):** Provides crisp tabular figures for CVE markers, memory registers, hashes, CVSS scores, and uppercase sovereign badge caps.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system implements a strict fluid 12-column architectural grid inspired by classical ledger proportions. 

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with `margin` of `2.5rem` and `gutter` of `1.5rem`. Maximum content container constrained to `1600px` to maintain optimal viewing angles on ultrawide command center displays.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `margin` of `1.5rem`, `gutter` of `1rem`. Secondary telemetry shifts from inline split panes to collapsible accordion cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-sm` of `1rem`, `gutter-sm` of `1rem`. Dense data tables convert into vertical ivory inspection cards with anchored metadata rows.

Vertical rhythm relies on balanced multi-step intervals: `space-xs` and `space-sm` for inline metadata pairing, `space-md` for internal panel padding, `space-lg` between distinct analytical modules, and `space-xl` for major ledger boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh digital drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layers paired with Delicate Parchment Shadows and Embossed Gilded Metalwork**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** Antique Cream (`#F5F0E6`), flat and matte.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Panels):** Alabaster White (`#FFFDFA`) surface bounded by a dual-stroke treatment: an inner 1px hairline border of muted gilded cream (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)`) and an exterior parchment shadow (`0 2px 8px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(197, 155, 39, 0.06)`).
- **Level 2 (Active Inspections & Flyout Drawers):** `#FFFDFA` surface backed by a warm bronze ambient aura (`0 12px 32px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08), 0 4px 12px rgba(184, 134, 11, 0.08)`), framed with a 1px solid antique gold perimeter (`#D4AF37`).
- **Level 3 (Modals & Severity Overlays):** Deep Imperial Midnight Navy backdrop scrim (`rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.65)`) with backdrop blur (`8px`), elevating pure cream panels with deep physical depth (`0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.25)`).
- **Embossed Accents:** Recessed code wells and tabular headers use an inset shadow (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05)`) cut into `#F1ECE1` surfaces to create the tactility of debossed letterpress.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Soft (`1`)** geometry. Radii are intentionally kept modest (4px for standard UI elements, 8px for cards and containers, 12px for system modals) to mimic hand-cut parchment, bespoke stationery, and structured leather goods. 

Pill shapes are reserved strictly for high-priority operational state indicators and CVSS severity tags to establish sharp visual contrast against the crisp rectangular framework of the dashboard ledger.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign Action:** Imperial Midnight Navy background (`#0B192C`), crisp cream serif or monospace text, bounded by a 1px inner hairline of gilded gold (`#D4AF37`). Hover introduces a gold glow box-shadow and deepens navy to `#132440`.
- **Secondary Remediation Action:** Alabaster white surface, framed with a 1.5px gilded brass border (`#D4AF37`), dark navy text. Hover floods the surface with warm antique gold wash (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`).
- **Critical Interlock (Revoke/Emergency Patch):** Crimson Lacquer background (`#9B111E`), ivory text, styled with an embossed border (`#C0392B`).

### Vulnerability Cards & Panels
Constructed on alabaster white (`#FFFDFA`) with an inset gold highlight line along the upper rim (a 2px sovereign accent bar in `#D4AF37` or `#9B111E` depending on severity). Content breaks cleanly with delicate `#E8DFD1` dividers.

### Chips & Severity Badges
- **Format:** JetBrains Mono uppercase, 11px, pill-shaped.
- **Critical (CVSS 9.0 - 10.0):** Rich Crimson Lacquer (`#9B111E`) background, crisp white monospace text, bounded by a subtle glowing border.
- **High/Medium:** Antique bronze tint (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`) with `#B8860B` typography.
- **Patched / Sealed:** Imperial Jade tint (`rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.12)`) with `#1B4D3E` typography.

### Data Tables & Vulnerability Lists
Zebra striping is strictly avoided. Rows sit on Alabaster White with 1px `#E8DFD1` underlines. Hovering over a vulnerability entry transitions the row background to a subtle antique silk wash (`#FAF6EE`) and reveals an active brass indicator pip on the left margin. Column headers display in uppercase mono with subtle tracking.

### Inputs & Inspection Selectors
Field backgrounds use debossed cream (`#FAF7F0`) with a 1px border of `#DCD3C3`. Focused states illuminate with a crisp 1px `#D4AF37` border and an ambient gold ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`). Monospaced placeholders in warm slate.

### Specialized SecOps Modules
- **Remediation Wax Seal Indicator:** Circular cryptographic stamp badge displaying overall deployment posture with an embossed double brass ring.
- **CVE Registry Terminal:** Integrated code and patch terminal enclosed in deep midnight navy (`#0B192C`) featuring gilded syntax highlights (`#D4AF37`) and cream command output (`#F5F0E6`).
